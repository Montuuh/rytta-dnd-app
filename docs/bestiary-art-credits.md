# Bestiary art credits

Bundled art for the SRD creatures (ADR 030). Generated by `scripts/bestiary/build_images.py`;
do not edit by hand. Every image was resized (720px) and cropped to a 128px square thumbnail.
The app's image viewer shows the same author, licence and source for each creature.

- Wikimedia Commons files keep the licence of each file, listed below.
- Battle for Wesnoth portraits (https://www.wesnoth.org), GNU GPL v2 or later, from commit
  `2b2a5fbac7144967fef33abaf303923e763e6bae` of https://github.com/wesnoth/wesnoth (`data/core/images/portraits`).
  The full source of the portraits is in that repository.
- game-icons.net icons (https://game-icons.net), CC BY 3.0, from commit
  `82d948812bfe3f269ef8f731dcdb07b08160edc4` of https://github.com/game-icons/icons, recoloured as ink on parchment.
- Rytta bestiary illustrations were painted for Rytta by its author with ChatGPT image
  generation (OpenAI, `gpt-image`) from the prompts of `art/bestiary/catalogue.csv`, then
  resized by `scripts/bestiary/import_art.py`. Under OpenAI's Terms of Use the output belongs to
  the author. Each source's checksum and C2PA provenance are in
  `scripts/bestiary/art-generated.json`.

  C2PA manifest (`ChatGPT` / `gpt-image`); one refused name, `glick.png`, which was the grick and was

## Design flow

1. Brief with `ui-ux-pro-max` (stat block for the DM at the table; reserve the square, load the 720px
   rendition, keep AC/HP/initiative in reach, no pill or button overuse).
2. Options page with interactive mockups and the real owlbear art
   (https://claude.ai/artifact/2odTnthjLD9c5wqPTi2TUP). The user answered P1 A (plate above the name),
   P2 B (two columns), P3 A (portrait in the combat header), P4 A (matted plate with photo corners),
   P5 A (tap opens the image, pencil changes it), all the recommended options.
3. `frontend-design` refinement: 12px mat in `--app-raised`, 16px accent corners (10px on the combat
   portrait), the viewer's print shadow and no card shadow, a slight dim of the print in dark themes,
   a 1px lift on hover and 0.99 press (none with reduced motion), focus outline around the mat, the
   pencil always visible (touch has no hover), sticky column 12px from the top (81px under the
   laptop's pinned actions).

## Implementation notes

- `ArtPlate` (print button + corners + overlay) in `creature-art.tsx`; `CreatureArtControl` shows it
  whenever the creature has an image, and the token picker otherwise. `CreaturePortrait` is the DM's
  combat header (dimmed when defeated, dashed when hidden); the viewer's change action is optional.
- The first image of a campaign creature moves the control from beside the name to the plate, which
  mounts it again: the save or remove notice and the focus now live in the stat block
  (`ArtNotice`), and the mutations resolve through their promise (TanStack skips `mutate` callbacks
  of an unmounted observer). Found by the RYT-043 e2e.
- Two columns from a 34rem frame (container query), stat block at least 20rem; abilities go to six
  columns from a 26rem block (container query instead of the viewport), so the laptop pane's narrower
  column keeps three.
- Labels: `bestiary.art.open` ("Ver la imagen de {{name}}") and `bestiary.art.edit` ("Cambiar la imagen
  de {{name}}"); `bestiary.art.trigger` and `bestiary.art.view` are gone with the token menu.

## Design review (impeccable)

- Critique and audit by two independent passes on the rendered captures (390 and 820, Hearth and
  Parchment, keyboard focus, 150% text, combat). The detector pass was clean; the heuristic pass scored
  about 31/40 and 13/20 before fixes.
- Fixed from it: the plate's width in pixels and screen height (larger text never enlarges the
  picture), the print shadow and hover lift on the print with its corners, the pencil busy rather than
  disabled while saving (focus stays), a surface band under the pencil's focus ring on light prints,
  every failed image address remembered (two broken images never swap), the quick stat block in the
  same two-column frame, the notice reset when another creature opens, an 88px combat portrait on
  phones. The reported combat Escape problem was a false alarm, now asserted in the e2e (Escape closes
  only the viewer, the detail stays open).
